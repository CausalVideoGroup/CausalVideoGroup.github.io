Formula components

Eleven SVG components typeset the FlowEdit ODE, DynaEdit guidance, and Bernini training objectives with MathJax 3.2.2. The public presentation uses the pre-rendered assets and does not load a MathJax CDN at runtime.

Example videos

Nine videos accompany the action-substitution, clothing-edit, and dog–bone comparisons. They are the presentation examples supplied by Peiyuan Zhu. The dog clips are 832 × 480, 25 fps, 81 frames; their static poster images show 2.40 s. Original video files are retained for playback.
