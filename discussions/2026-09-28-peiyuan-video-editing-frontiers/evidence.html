Qualitative editing examples

Action substitution

The requested change replaces walking toward the door with drinking water. In the shown Gemini Omni example, drinking is added near the end while much of the walking remains. Check both the presence of the target event and removal of the source event. View the example.

Clothing edits across turns

The sequence edits clothing colors, preserves the edits in the next turn, then restores both colors. It does not test undoing only one change while preserving another. View the sequence.

Dog–bone interaction

The source contains a yellow-brown bone on the ground. The archived target has a differently shaped white bone in the mouth from the first frame, so it does not demonstrate the original bone being picked up. The third clip is LucyEdit: the original bone stays on the ground and the dog's coat changes. The fourth clip, newly supplied by the presenter, is Gemini Omni Flash: the dog approaches the original bone near the end, but the clip does not show it lifting and carrying the bone. Attribution follows the presenter's correction. These four clips belong to one example. Use Play all to start the comparison together and Pause all to pause it. View the comparison.

These examples diagnose event timing, object continuity, interaction execution, and preservation. They do not establish overall model rankings or failure rates.

Source-as-output deliberately performs no edit and is excluded from the editor ranking. High preservation or smoothness cannot establish action completion. In the Omni sensitivity study, switching the same Qwen3-VL-32B judge from 12 sampled frames to all decoded frames changes the no-edit Overall score from 17.608 to 33.340. The direction of change also depends on judge size, so more frames are not automatically a more accurate evaluation.

Local configuration names

FM-only denotes a Bernini-R 1.3B adapter trained with direct flow-matching supervision and source-plus-instruction inference. Zebra denotes a reference-conditioned controller whose pipeline additionally generates a pseudo-reference. Neither name denotes an independently published paper. Their extra inputs and generation cost must be reported when comparing results; neither has a full-video record in the frozen Omni comparison used here.
