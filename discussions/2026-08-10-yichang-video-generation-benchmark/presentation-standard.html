08 · VLM-as-judge

VLMs broaden the scope of judgment while increasing protocol dependence

Are the same person's face, clothing, and body structure still consistent one minute later?Such attribute-level questions are closer to human descriptions than CLIP similarity, but depend more heavily on the judge's language and vision capabilities.
Which frames?Uniform, event-adjacent, endpoints, or segment-wise sampling?
How to ask?Absolute scores, binary questions, attribute gates, or pairwise comparisons?
Which version?Model updates can assign different scores to the same video.
How to calibrate?Align with human rankings, or consider only mean correlation?
Evidence boundary Self-Forcing++ validates its judge with two paper authors × 20 prompts; this supports ranking within that sample, but does not establish general validity.

14 · Three families of 3D evaluation

Methods for evaluating 3D consistency fall into three families

Image-Correspondence Constraints

Establish pixel or feature correspondences across frames and test whether they satisfy a shared camera geometry.

Epipolar constraint · Sampson error · reprojection error
Representative: WorldScore

Depth / Point-Cloud Geometry

Estimate depth, point maps, and cameras, then project multiple views into a shared 3D coordinate system for comparison.

Point-cloud alignment · cross-view warping · MEt3R
Representative: MEt3R

Per-Video 3DGS Reconstruction

Fit 3DGS to a generated video, then validate reconstruction consistency on views withheld from fitting.

3DGS · held-out-view PSNR / SSIM / LPIPS
Representative: Cosmos-Predict1
Shared boundary DINO / CLIP similarity and VLM judgments can provide supplementary evidence, but they do not constitute measurements of 3D geometry.

Speaker Notes

Glossary

Metric lineage
The algorithmic and protocol origin of a metric, such as VBench, a paper-specific custom metric, or an environment-task score.
Prompt lineage
The origin of input content and selection rules, such as MovieGenBench, official VBench prompts, or custom trajectories.
Proxy metric
A measurable signal used to approximate a target capability, such as DINO feature similarity as a proxy for identity consistency.
3D consistency
Whether different viewpoints can be explained by the same spatial geometry.
Revisit consistency
Whether a scene or state is correctly restored after leaving and returning to a prior location.
Out-of-sight consistency
Whether hidden state continues to evolve correctly while an object is invisible and is rendered correctly when the object reappears.
Sampson error
An approximate epipolar-geometry error derived from feature correspondences and the fundamental matrix.
3DGS
3D Gaussian Splatting. A 3D representation is fitted to generated video and then validated for reconstructability on held-out views.
MEt3R
Evidence of geometric consistency based on cross-view projection of point clouds.
