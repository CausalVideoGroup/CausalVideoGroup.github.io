Video Reasoning: One Year of Debate in 26 Papers

Led by Yichang Jian

Give a video model a first frame and a text instruction, ask it to draw the solution, and score the result objectively.

A 39-slide Chinese deck that covers 26 papers in publication order, from the first zero-shot claims (2025-09) to controlled scaling studies (2026-09). Every paper slide shows its key numbers as a chart or a paper figure, and the closing slide answers four questions with links back to the evidence.

Discussion focus

The field argued about four things: whether the video model or a language model is doing the reasoning, what counts as a correct answer, whether reasoning happens across frames or across denoising steps, and whether synthetic data can teach it. The deck follows those arguments paper by paper.
